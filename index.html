Rank Tracker

Overwatch 2 · who can you queue with

Ranks come from public Blizzard career profiles via the OverFast API. A profile must be set to public in Overwatch 2 for its ranks to appear, and Blizzard’s profile data updates on a delay — it is not live.

Grouping verdicts follow Blizzard’s wide-group rules: more than 5 skill divisions apart makes a Diamond-and-below group wide, 3 if anyone is Master, and any group with a Grandmaster or Champion is always wide (capped at 2 players). Wide groups still queue — they just face other wide groups, wait longer and earn less. Blizzard adjusts these between seasons; see their competitive update.
